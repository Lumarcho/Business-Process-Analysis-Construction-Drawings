# Recommendations

## 1. Digital Document Management

Introduce a centralised approach for storing, accessing and managing construction drawings.

## 2. Improved Version Control

Implement consistent document naming, versioning and approval practices.

## 3. Digital Approval Workflows

Introduce structured workflows for reviewing and approving drawings.

## 4. Cloud Collaboration

Use appropriate digital collaboration tools to reduce dependency on physical document distribution.

## 5. Technology Improvements

Address outdated computers, software and printing/scanning infrastructure.

## 6. Process Standardisation

Develop standard procedures for drawing preparation, review, approval and distribution.

## 7. Training

Provide appropriate training to ensure users can effectively adopt improved technologies and processes.

## Overall Improvement Direction

The recommended approach combines:

**Technology + Process + People + Governance**

This creates a more sustainable approach to business process improvement.
